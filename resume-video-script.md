## Recording Tips for Resume Video

1. **Dress:** Professional or smart casual
2. **Background:** Clean, neutral (home office or plain wall)
3. **Framing:** Head and shoulders, centered
4. **Lighting:** Well-lit face, no harsh shadows
5. **Audio:** Clear, no background noise
6. **Eye Contact:** Look at camera lens, not screen
7. **Energy:** Confident, enthusiastic but not over the top
8. **Ending:** Slight nod and smile after "Thank you"
