# Intro Video Scripts

Choose one of these 3 variations for your 15-30 second intro video.

---

## Version 1: Professional & Direct
**Duration:** ~15 seconds

```
Hi, I'm Awais — a Full-Stack Developer specializing in
Shopify app development and the MERN stack.

I build production-ready applications that solve real
business problems.

Let's connect and build something great together.
```

---

## Version 2: Story-Driven
**Duration:** ~20 seconds

```
Hey, I'm Awais.

Over the past 3 years, I've built Shopify apps,
real-time platforms, and web applications for
clients worldwide.

From speech recognition systems to e-commerce solutions —
I turn ideas into working products.

Check out my work, and let's talk.
```

---

## Version 3: Friendly & Conversational
**Duration:** ~25 seconds

```
Hey there! I'm Syed Awais — a Full-Stack Developer
based in Pakistan.

I specialize in building Shopify apps — both public
and private — along with web applications using
React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.

Whether it's a SaaS platform or a custom business tool,
I love solving complex problems with clean code.

Feel free to explore my projects and reach out!
```

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Pace:** Speak naturally, not too fast
2. **Tone:** Confident but friendly
3. **Eye Contact:** Look directly at the camera
4. **Smile:** Start and end with a genuine smile
5. **Background:** Clean, professional (plain wall or office)
6. **Lighting:** Face a window or light source
7. **Audio:** Quiet environment, speak clearly

---

## Quick Practice Lines

Before recording, practice these openers:
- "Hi, I'm Awais..."
- "Hey, I'm Awais..."
- "Hey there! I'm Syed Awais..."

Pick the one that feels most natural to you.
